Once a resident of the Weaver’s Woods until she ended up here with
an injury, Spider has since become a welcome resident and sworn off
of eating people… for now. Exceptionally quiet, she could do with
some help coming out of her shell so to speak.

Spider loves riddles and can make beautiful web art.

Tasks:
• Share riddles and puzzles
• Convince another familiar to visit her
• Take a message to the Weaver Woods in Glimmerwood Grove
• Clean out her old webs
• Find where she’s hiding

Ability: Web Trap – Spider sets up a web near your cottage. Once per
Ailment, draw a card and gain an ANIMAL Reagent equal to or
less than the card’s value.
