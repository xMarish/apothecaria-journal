**Type:** Adventurer  
**Race:** Human  
**Job:** Cartographer

Rowan maps paths through places that have a habit of rearranging themselves. He wears a weather-beaten green cloak, has ink-stained fingertips, and carries a brass map case with a dent he insists is shaped like a duck.

He's careful about his work and hopeless at asking for help. By the time he knocks at [[The Open Eye Apothecary]], he's already walked past twice trying to decide whether to come in.

> “Are you Mirabel? The baker sent me. He said if I came back a third time to ask where the apothecary was, he'd walk me here himself.”

While Mirabel examines him, Rowan spots [[Morrow]] outside. He unfolds his map and points out a patch of wild apples on the road into [[Rannoc Village]].
