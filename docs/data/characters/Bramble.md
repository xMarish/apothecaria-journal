Bramble is a young bog troll who sorts his lost buttons into little jars by colour. He insists the shiny ones are coins.

Just before closing, he knocks on the window of [[The Open Eye Apothecary]] with one knuckle. The last time he tried a door handle in Rannoc, it came off in his hand.

When [[Mirabel Hollow]] asks what brings him in, he sets a jar of mismatched buttons on the counter.

“I can pay. The green ones are worth more.”
