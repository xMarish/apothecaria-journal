## About Mirabel

Mirabel Hollow runs the Open Eye Apothecary, where she treats patients and brews remedies from reagents she gathers. She’s composed and observant, with a playful streak that shows once she feels at ease.

## First week in the journal

In [[Y1 Spring Week 01]], Mirabel treated Felux Dawncrest for Magnetic Thumb and met [[Morrow]] through a calling ritual.
