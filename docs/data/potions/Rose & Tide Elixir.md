**Name:** Rose & Tide Elixir

**Ailment:** Unrecorded in the recipe.

**Ingredients:** Wild Rose ★★, Fossil Fish ★★★, Fairy Dust ★★, Mermaid’s Gift ★★, and optional Moonstone.

**Tools:** Mortar and pestle, cauldron.

**Preparation instructions:** Heat Mermaid’s Gift in the cauldron, adding Moonstone if you have it. While it comes to a boil, crush the Wild Rose seeds and Fossil Fish scales. After boiling for a few minutes, take it off the heat and let it cool. Remove the Moonstone if used. Stir in the crushed seeds and scales, then add Fairy Dust and stir again.

**Usage instructions:** Serve once cooled and stirred. The brew may carry Poison if you skipped the Moonstone.
