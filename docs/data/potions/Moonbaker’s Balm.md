**Name:** Moonbaker’s Balm

**Ailment:** Baker's Blisters ★

**Ingredients:** Moon Moss ★★★, Moon Lotus ★★

**Tools:** Mortar and pestle

**Preparation instructions:** Pound the Moon Moss into a soft paste, then fold in crushed Moon Lotus petals.

**Usage instructions:** Spread a thin layer over the cooled blisters.
