**Name:** Wayfarer’s Mending Draught

**Ailment:** Wounded

**Tags:** Wound ★, Pain ★

**Ingredients:** Hermit Snail ★, Hiker’s Helper ★, Sun Sugar

**Tool:** Cauldron

**Preparation instructions:** Put the cauldron on high heat. Add the Hermit Snail and Hiker’s Helper and bring to a boil. Add Sun Sugar for sweetness.
