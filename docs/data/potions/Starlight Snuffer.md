**Name:** Starlight Snuffer

**Ailment:** Star Struck ★

**Ingredients:** Wisp Fire ★, Hermit Snail ★

**Tools:** Mortar and pestle, cauldron

**Preparation instructions:** Crush the Hermit Snail shell and stir the powder into a Wisp Fire infusion until the glow fades.

**Usage instructions:** Serve once the draught has cooled and gone dark.

Recorded in [[Y1 Spring Week 10]]. Tools and instructions are suggested in-world details.
