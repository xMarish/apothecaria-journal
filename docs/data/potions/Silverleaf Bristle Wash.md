**Name:** Silverleaf Bristle Wash

**Ailment:** Pollen Pox ★

**Ingredients:** Hair of Boar ★★, Silverleaf ★

**Tools:** Mortar and pestle, strainer

**Preparation instructions:** Bruise the Silverleaf, steep it with the boar bristles and strain the cooled wash.

**Usage instructions:** Dab the wash onto the itchy spots.
