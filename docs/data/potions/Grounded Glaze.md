**Name:** Grounded Glaze

**Ailment:** Hover Hives ★

**Ingredients:** Smooth-Croak ★, Milkstone ★★

**Tools:** Mortar and pestle, mixing bowl

**Preparation instructions:** Powder the Milkstone and blend it with the Smooth-Croak reagent to make a spreadable glaze.

**Usage instructions:** Dab onto the hovering hives and let the glaze set.
