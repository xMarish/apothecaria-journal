**Name:** Guppy Spark Draught

**Ailment:** Magnetic Thumb ★

**Ingredients:** Shock Fish ★★, Golden Scale Guppy ★★

**Tools:** Mortar and pestle, cauldron

**Preparation instructions:** Crush a golden guppy scale. Stir the powder into a warm Shock Fish infusion and let the sparks settle.

**Usage instructions:** Serve a small cup once the draught has stopped crackling.
