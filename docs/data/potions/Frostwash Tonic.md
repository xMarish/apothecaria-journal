**Name:** Frostwash Tonic

**Ailment:** Cludgie Mouth ★

**Ingredients:** Glittersnow ★★, Slime Shell ★★

**Tools:** Mortar and pestle, mixing bowl

**Preparation instructions:** Grind the Slime Shell to a paste and stir in the Glittersnow until the wash turns pearly.

**Usage instructions:** Swish the chilled tonic, then spit it out.
